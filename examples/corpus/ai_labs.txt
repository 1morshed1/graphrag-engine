Sam Altman leads OpenAI, an artificial intelligence lab based in San Francisco. Sam Altman studied at Stanford University. OpenAI developed GPT, a large language model. Microsoft invested in OpenAI heavily.

Demis Hassabis co-founded DeepMind, an AI research lab. Demis Hassabis studied at Cambridge. DeepMind built AlphaFold, a protein folding model. Google acquired DeepMind.

OpenAI uses large clusters of GPUs to train models. DeepMind uses reinforcement learning to develop agents. Both OpenAI and DeepMind are located in major technology hubs.
